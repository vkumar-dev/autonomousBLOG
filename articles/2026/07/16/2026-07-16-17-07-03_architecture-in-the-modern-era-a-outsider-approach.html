The Genesis of Architectural Myths

For millennia, architectural myths have shaped our understanding of the built environment. From Icarus' flight inspiring Gothic spires to Pyramid builders as celestial navigators, these stories weave a rich tapestry of beliefs and ideologies. But as we venture into the modern era, it's time to reevaluate these narratives.

Did you know? The term "Corinthian" refers not to the Greek city but to a muse in Ovid's Metamorphoses.

The Outsider's Perspective: Shattering the Status Quo

As outsiders peering into the world of architecture, we find a profession where innovation and tradition clash, and where creativity can be stifled by the weight of history. But it's precisely this tension that drives architectural progress.

Modern Masters: Breaking the Mold

In the modern era, architects like Frank Gehry and Zaha Hadid have shattered traditional norms. Their buildings are not just functional structures, but living narratives that challenge our preconceptions about form and function.
