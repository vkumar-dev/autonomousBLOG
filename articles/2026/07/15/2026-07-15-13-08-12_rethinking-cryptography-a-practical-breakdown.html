The Future: Quantum Cryptography

Quantum cryptography, or quantum key distribution (QKD), is a revolutionary method of communication that utilizes the principles of quantum mechanics to create uncrackable codes. By leveraging photons—the fundamental particles of light—quantum cryptography promises security like never before.
