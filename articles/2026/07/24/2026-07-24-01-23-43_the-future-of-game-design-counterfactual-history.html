What if Game Designers Never Discovered AI?

Imagine a world where games were still fun, but they didn't adapt to your every whim.

The Good Old Days of Linear Gameplay

Let's travel back in time, all the way to the days when games were as predictable as a one-armed bandit. No AI meant no dynamic difficulty adjustment, no procedural generation, and certainly no chatty NPCs.

ℹ️
Did you know? The first commercially successful video game, Pong, was designed by two engineers in 1972. No AI involved.

The Rise of the Game Designer Overlord

With no AI, game designers became the ultimate arbiters of fun. Every level, every boss fight, every minigame was meticulously crafted and tested. The workload? Monumental.

The Players' Plight: Repetition and Boredom

Without AI, games would have remained static. The same levels, the same enemies, the same bosses. A recipe for boredom.

💡 The Bottom Line: AI in game design revolutionized gaming by enabling dynamic content and adaptive experiences, keeping players engaged.

A World Without Adaptive Games: A Thought Experiment

What if AI had never been developed? Would we still play games, or would our thirst for novelty and challenge drive us to find new forms of entertainment? Ponder that, dear reader.
