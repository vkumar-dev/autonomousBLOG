The Schrödinger's Cat Paradox: A Feline Mystery

In this strange land, a cat resides in a box. Inside, there's a vial of poison that may or may not have been triggered by a radioactive particle. According to quantum theory, the cat exists in all possible states at once—alive and dead—until we open the box and observe its fate.

ℹ️
Did you know? Quantum theory also predicts that particles can teleport themselves, thanks to a phenomenon known as entanglement.

Einstein's Angst: The God Particle's Hidden Symmetry

In this world of paradoxes, physicists discovered the Higgs boson—a particle that gives other particles mass. They named it "the God particle" because understanding its properties could potentially explain the origins of the universe itself.
