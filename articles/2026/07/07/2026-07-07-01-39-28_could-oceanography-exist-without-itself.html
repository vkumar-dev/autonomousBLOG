From the ancient Polynesians navigating by the stars to the Vikings charting their course with sunstones, humanity has sought to conquer the aquatic expanse. And yet, it was not until the Age of Exploration that we truly began to understand its enigmatic nature.

The Drowned Library of Alexandria

Imagine a world without Copernicus' celestial maps, without Galileo's telescope, and without Newton's gravitational equations. Now, imagine a world without the knowledge of ocean currents, tides, and marine life. It is a chilling thought experiment that forces us to confront our reliance on these unsung heroes.
