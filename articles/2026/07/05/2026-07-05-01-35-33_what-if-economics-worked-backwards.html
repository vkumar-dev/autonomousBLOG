The Value Vortex

Our beloved products, instead of chasing consumers' wallets, are drawn irresistibly towards them. They're like moths to a flame, except the flame is disposable income.

The Inverse Auction House

Art galleries, instead of bidding for the most expensive pieces, bid for the least. The higher the price, the less desirable the art becomes.

💡 The Bottom Line: In this world, the rules of supply and demand are flipped on their head. The economy is a chaotic dance where everything moves in reverse. But could it work? Maybe. After all, who said money should always chase value?
