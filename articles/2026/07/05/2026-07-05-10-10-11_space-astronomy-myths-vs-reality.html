In Space, No One Can Hear Your Myths

So let's set the record straight. Here's a cosmic fact-check for the age of social media.

The Universe, Flat as a Pancake

You've probably heard it before: the universe is flat. Well, not like your breakfast pancake, but flat nonetheless. But this is one myth that needs debunking with a hefty dose of reality.

ℹ️
Did you know? The universe's flatness is measured in something called the "flatness problem." Sounds like a sci-fi mystery, doesn't it?

Astronauts, Floating Around Weightless

In zero gravity, astronauts don't float around like they're in a bubble bath. Instead, they "float" because they're not pushing off anything to stay grounded.

Aliens, Abounding Everywhere

We don't know if we're alone in the universe. But here's a thought experiment: If there are billions of galaxies, each with billions of stars, and some of those stars have planets... well, you do the math.

Comets, Tail Chasers

Contrary to popular belief, comets don't chase their tails. Instead, it's the solar wind that creates a glowing wake behind them.

The Stars, Twinkling Because of Magic

Nope, twinkling stars aren't caused by fairy dust or a sprinkle of stardust. Instead, it's due to Earth's atmosphere refracting the light.

Black Holes, Cosmic Vacuums

Black holes aren't empty voids. They're super dense regions of space where gravity is so strong that nothing—not even light—can escape.

The Moon, Made of Green Cheese

Sorry to burst your bubble (or cheese wheel), but the moon isn't made of green cheese. It's primarily composed of silicate minerals, and it's as far from a dairy product as you can get.

The Cosmic Puzzle

Our fascination with space is as vast and complex as the universe itself. But when we embrace reality over myth, we uncover deeper truths about our place in the cosmos.
