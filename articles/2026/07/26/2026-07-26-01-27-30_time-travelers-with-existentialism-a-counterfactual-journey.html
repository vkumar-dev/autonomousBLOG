The Time Travelers Who Brought Us Existential Crisis

Imagine a world where existential crises are as common as the common cold. Thanks to time travelers, that's exactly what we might have ended up with.

A Brief History of Existentialism

Existentialism, the philosophy that emphasizes individual existence, freedom, and choice, has its roots in the mid-19th century. But it wasn't until the 20th century that philosophers like Jean-Paul Sartre and Friedrich Nietzsche brought it to the forefront.

ℹ️
Did you know? Sartre's most famous work, "No Exit," was actually a play about the joys of being stuck in traffic.

The Unlikely Suspects

Now, imagine time travelers arriving from the future. Armed with a singular goal: to spread existentialism like a virulent meme.

The Existential Pandemic

As they crisscrossed history, these time travelers would sow the seeds of doubt and uncertainty. Philosophy classes were filled with students asking "What is the meaning of life?" instead of "What's for dinner?"

💡 The Bottom Line: If time travelers had their way, we might all be living in a world where nihilism is the national pastime.
