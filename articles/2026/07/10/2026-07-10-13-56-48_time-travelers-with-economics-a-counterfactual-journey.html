Welcome to a World Where Time Travelers are Everywhere, and So Is Inflation

Imagine stepping into the shower to find a man from the future selling you Bitcoin at a discount. Welcome to our new reality.

The Time Traveling Economy

In this counterfactual world, time travel has become as commonplace as the internet was in the late '90s. The economic implications are staggering, and not entirely pleasant.

ℹ️
Did you know? Time travelers have caused a spike in demand for vintage clothing. The 1960s are experiencing an economic boom.

The Scramble for Resources

With the ability to travel through time, resources can be harvested from any era. This has led to rampant resource depletion and a race for control over history's riches.

The Impact on Labor Markets

With the ability to travel back in time, labor markets are being disrupted. Time travelers can work in any era, leading to a surplus of skilled workers and increasing unemployment.

💡 The Bottom Line: The influx of time travelers has created a labor market paradox, leading to widespread unemployment and economic instability.

Pondering the Future of Time Travel

As we grapple with the economic consequences of time travel, it's important to consider how we can regulate this technology for the benefit of all. Can we create a time-travel economy that works for everyone? Or will we be forever trapped in a cycle of resource depletion and labor disruption?
