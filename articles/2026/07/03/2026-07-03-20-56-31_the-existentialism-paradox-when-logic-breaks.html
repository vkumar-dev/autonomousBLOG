Welcome to the Asylum of Absurdity: Your Ticket to Existentialism

Where else can you find a philosophical movement that champions the absurdity of life, and wears it like a badge of honor?

The Circus of Existentialism: A Quick Introduction

Imagine a world where every choice matters, and yet, none does. Where we are free to make our own destiny, but destined to be unhappy. Welcome to the existentialist playground.

ℹ️
Did you know? Existentialism was born out of the philosophical crisis brought about by World War II. Philosophers like Jean-Paul Sartre and Martin Heidegger sought to make sense of an increasingly chaotic world.

The Elephant in the Room: Economic Reality

In our capitalist reality, existentialism becomes a luxury few can afford. We are encouraged to chase meaning and happiness through material success, rather than confronting the absurdity of existence head-on.

The Absurd Dichotomy: Freedom and Meaninglessness

Existentialism posits that life has no inherent meaning, yet we are responsible for creating our own. This paradox is a punch in the face to those who seek a neat, tidy worldview.

The Final Bow: A Question for the Reader
