Welcome to the World of Design Dominance

Imagine a world where designers have the final say in what artists create. Sounds like a dystopian nightmare, right? But what if it's not so different from our own?

The Designer-Artist Hierarchy

In this alternate universe, designers have taken the reins of artistic expression. They've turned the creative process into a strategic game of "What sells best?" Instead of artists experimenting with new styles and techniques, they're expected to follow design trends dictated by market research.

ℹ️
Did you know? The term "designer" was first used in the 18th century to describe people who made high-quality, expensive clothing. Today, it encompasses a much broader field.

The Art of Marketing

Artists are now marketing professionals first, creators second. They're tasked with understanding consumer behavior and creating works that appeal to specific demographics. The result? A homogenized artistic landscape where originality is a rare commodity.

The Rise of the DIY Artist

But not all is lost. The rise of the designer-driven art world has led to a counterculture movement of DIY artists who reject market trends and focus on personal expression. These artists find their audience through social media, where they can connect with like-minded individuals.

💡 The Bottom Line: Even in a world dominated by design, artists find ways to express themselves. The key is to embrace the DIY spirit and create work that speaks to you.

What If We Flipped the Script?

So, what if we flipped the script? What if artists called the shots in the world of design? It's an intriguing thought experiment that challenges us to reconsider the balance of power in creative industries.

Pondering Question: If artists had control over design, how would it change the way we live and interact with technology?
