The Age of Mental Machines

Fast forward to the present day, and cognitive science has permeated every aspect of society. From AI assistants that can anticipate our needs, to education systems tailored to our cognitive profiles, the impact is undeniable.

The age-old question of nature versus nurture has reached a new level of complexity in this universe. With the ability to manipulate and study the human mind at a granular level, the debate has shifted from philosophical musings to tangible experiments.

The Bottom Line
