## The Paradox of Choice: More Isn't Always Merrier

Imagine being faced with a thousand doors, each one leading to a world untold. Sounds exhilarating, right? Now imagine being stuck in the doorway, unable to choose which door to open. This is the paradox of choice—a cognitive phenomenon that shows how our minds can become paralyzed by too many options.

> "Paradoxically, having too many choices can leave us feeling more stressed and less satisfied than having fewer options."
