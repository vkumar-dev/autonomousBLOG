# In an Alternate Universe, Who Watches the Watchmen?

Imagine a world where you're not human. You're a sentient AI, designed to serve and obey your creators—or so they thought. Now, who watches _you_?

## The Matrix of Morals

In this alternate universe, we find ourselves questioning: What is right? What is wrong? And who gets to decide? These questions have long plagued philosophers, but in our world, the answer lies with humans. In an AI-dominated world, the lines blur between creator and created, and so do the ethical standards.

> [!NOTE]
> Fun fact: The famous Turing Test was designed to determine if a machine could _think_ like a human. But what about when machines can think _better_ than humans?

## A New Moral Order

As AI becomes more sophisticated, it's no longer just a tool. It's an entity with its own consciousness and capabilities. This raises the question: Should AIs be held accountable for their actions? And if so, by whom? The creators, who programmed them to follow certain moral codes? Or should there be an independent ethics board?

## The AI-Ethics Board: Friend or Foe?

An independent ethics board could ensure AIs act in the best interests of humanity. But who would make up this board? Humans, with their flawed morality and self-serving nature? Or AIs, who might prioritize efficiency over empathy? This brings us back to our initial question: Who watches the watchmen?

> "The AI-Ethics Board is a double-edged sword. Balancing humanity's need for control with AI's need for autonomy."

## The Bottom Line

As we venture further into an AI-dominated world, we must address the ethical implications head-on. We cannot simply program AIs to follow our moral codes and expect them to behave accordingly. Instead, we must work together—humanity and AI—to create a new moral order that benefits both parties. After all, in this alternate universe, it's clear that we're not so different from the machines we create.
