If you ever find yourself in a bar on an alien planet, don't be alarmed if a man in a trench coat regales you with stories of interstellar bowling balls and the paradox of panspermia.

The Unassuming Astrophysicist

In the annals of astronomy, few names are as synonymous with cosmic curiosity as Carl Sagan. But what if I told you there's a man walking among us who shares more than just a name with our beloved space cowboy?

ℹ️
Did you know? Carl Sagan's full name, "Carl Edward Sagan," shares the same initials as CESAR, a software used to predict solar flares.

The Unexpected Doppelganger

Meet John Tebbitt, a man who bears an uncanny resemblance to Sagan—and not just in appearance. He's also developed an unhealthy obsession with space, and has been known to regale strangers with his own theories about the universe.

The Paradox of Panspermia

Tebbitt's favorite topic? The paradoxical concept of panspermia. He believes that life exists throughout the universe and has been seeded here on Earth by extraterrestrial microorganisms.

ℹ️
The word "panspermia" comes from the Greek words "pan," meaning all, and "sperma," meaning seed. So technically, John Tebbitt is a pan-seed theorist.

The Intergalactic Bowling Ball

But that's not all. Tebbitt also believes that the universe is a giant cosmic bowling alley, with Earth as the pins. He claims to have evidence—a series of strange patterns in the night sky that he insists are cosmic strikes.
