The Ethical Minefield of Game Design

As I began to design my game, I was faced with a seemingly simple question: What kind of game do I want to create? But as I delved deeper into the process, I realized that this wasn't just about deciding between platformers and RPGs. It was about making choices that would impact real people—players and non-players alike.

The Dark Side of Fun: Exploring the Ethical Ramifications of Game Design Choices

From microtransactions that encourage compulsive spending to violent content that desensitizes players, game designers wield a great deal of power. But with this power comes a responsibility to consider the potential consequences of our creations.

💡 The Bottom Line: Game design isn't just about creating entertaining experiences—it's about crafting worlds that challenge, engage, and ultimately improve our understanding of ourselves and the world around us.

The Mirror Reflects Us: The Ethics of Gaming as a Mirror to Society

As we continue to push the boundaries of what games can be, it's essential that we remain mindful of the ethical implications of our choices. For in crafting these digital worlds, we are not merely creating escapes from reality—we are reflecting it, distorted and magnified, back upon ourselves.
