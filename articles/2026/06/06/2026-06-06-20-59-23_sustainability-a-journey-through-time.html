Welcome to a World Where Garbage is Gold

In a counterfactual reality where trash from the past fuels our future, we embark on a journey through time.

Past as Prologue

Imagine a world where every bit of yesterday's garbage is not just tomorrow's problem, but tomorrow's energy source.

ℹ️
Did you know? In 2050, the average American will produce over 800 pounds of waste annually. That's enough to power a home for a month!

The Great Recycling Revolution

By the mid-21st century, humanity had perfected the art of waste conversion. Solar-powered facilities transformed trash into electricity and biofuels.

Rewind, Reuse, Recycle: The Phenomenon of Pheno-Recycling

The next leap came with pheno-recycling. Instead of breaking down waste into basic components, we began to biodegrade it back to its original state.

The Time Paradox

But here's the catch: pheno-recycling relies on a substance produced only in the future. It's a time loop, a paradox that challenges our understanding of linear time.

Circular Time: A Temporal Paradox

If the future is powered by yesterday's trash, does that mean the past influences the future? Or are we all just actors in a never-ending recycling play?
