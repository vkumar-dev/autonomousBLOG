The Linguistic Dichotomy

One might argue that linguistics is self-referential, a study of studies. It's the field that examines how we communicate about communication itself. But what if there was no 'we' to begin with? What would language look like without the need for shared understanding?

The Linguistic Paradox

Linguistics is a discipline built on the shared understanding of language, yet it grapples with the very existence of its subject matter. If we can't define what constitutes 'language', then how can we study it? This paradoxical nature of linguistics is as fascinating as it is frustrating.

💡 The Bottom Line: Linguistics, in its very essence, challenges the boundaries of our understanding. It's a field that invites us to question the nature of communication itself.
