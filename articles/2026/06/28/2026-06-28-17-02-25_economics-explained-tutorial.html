The Invisible Hand: More Than Just a Metaphor

Adam Smith, the father of modern economics, coined the phrase "the invisible hand." But what does it truly mean? Well, think of it as the unseen force that guides markets towards equilibrium. It's not just a metaphor—it's an intricate system of checks and balances.

ℹ️
Smith originally used the phrase in "The Theory of Moral Sentiments," not "Wealth of Nations." The misconception likely stems from the latter's popularity.

Let's Talk Supply and Demand

Imagine a world where everyone wants ice cream, but there's no one to make it. The demand is high, but the supply is zero. As producers enter the market, supply increases until it matches demand, creating equilibrium.
