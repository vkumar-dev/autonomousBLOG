An Unintuitive Harvest

In a seemingly surreal turn of events, the humble cornfield has become the battleground for technological innovation. Gone are the days when farmers donned their overalls and waded through knee-deep mud to harvest their crops. Now, a swarm of drones, powered by solar energy and equipped with precision machinery, patrol Iowa's cornfields, turning the traditional farming model on its head.

Did you know? The first drone to harvest a crop was developed by Blue River Technology in 2017. Since then, the technology has evolved rapidly, with companies like John Deere investing heavily in this field.

The AI Economist: A New Breed

Meanwhile, in the world of economics, a new breed of experts has emerged. They're called econometricians, and they use complex mathematical models to predict economic trends. But even these experts are finding it hard to keep up with the ever-evolving AI algorithms that can predict market fluctuations with alarming accuracy.
