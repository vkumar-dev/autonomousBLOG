The Strata of Simulated Time

As we venture deeper into the digital realm, geologists find themselves navigating a landscape as unstable as quicksand. The flow of time is not linear but cyclical; past, present, and future coexist in an eternal now.

The Unconformity of Digital Layers

In the physical world, geological layers are neatly stacked and unyielding. But in our digital reality, they're a messy amalgamation of outdated code, discarded ideas, and forgotten dreams. The real challenge for the digital geologist is not to find solid rock—it's to decipher the chaos.

The Fracture of Reality

As our digital world grows more complex, so too does the risk of fractures and faults. A single errant keystroke could cause a cascading failure that reshapes continents or even collapses entire dimensions.
