Meet the Socrates You Didn't Know - The Unknowable Known

Born in Athens, died from hemlock, and yet, who was this man that made philosophers a household name? A closer look at Socrates might just challenge your understanding of knowing.

The Philosopher Who Never Wrote

Imagine a philosopher who didn't write any books, yet is considered one of the most influential thinkers in Western history. Meet Socrates, the man who lived by questioning everything - including himself.

ℹ️
Did you know? Socrates was sentenced to death for "corrupting the youth" of Athens!

The Method of Socratic Inquiry

Socrates' method, known as Socratic inquiry or dialectic, is a form of conversation used to stimulate critical thinking and to draw out ideas and underlying presumptions. But can this very method reveal its own limits?

The Unknowable Known

Socrates' paradox, or the "unknown known," refers to things we are not aware we know. Could it be that our understanding of Socrates himself falls into this category? A thought-provoking notion indeed.

💡 The Bottom Line: Socrates' life and teachings challenge us to question our own knowledge, inviting us to explore the boundaries of what we can truly know.

Pondering Plato

As we delve deeper into Socrates' work, as told by his most famous student, Plato, one can't help but wonder: how much of Plato's interpretation is Socratic wisdom... and how much is Plato's interpretation? A fascinating dilemma indeed.

Pondering Question: If the philosopher who questioned everything can still be shrouded in mystery, what does that say about our own understanding of the world?
