The Uncomputable Melodies: A Brief History

In 1936, the brilliant mathematician Alan Turing posed a question: Can machines think? His answer, the Turing Test, was a benchmark for the ability of machines to mimic human intelligence. But what about something even more elusive: human creativity? The world of music, with its infinite possibilities and complexities, has long been considered the ultimate frontier for artificial intelligence.
