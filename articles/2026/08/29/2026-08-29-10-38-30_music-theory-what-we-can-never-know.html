The Art of the Absurd: The Circle of Fifths

The Circle of Fifths is like a map of a parallel universe. It's a beautiful, ordered diagram that somehow manages to be both intuitive and utterly baffling. It's a perfect example of how music theory can make you question everything you know about reality.

The Rule of Three: Chords You'll Never Forget

If you've ever tried to write a song, you know the struggle of finding the right chords. But fear not, for music theory has graced us with the glorious Rule of Three. It's a simple principle that promises that if you find three chords that sound good together, you've got a hit on your hands. But why does it work? Nobody knows, and that's the beauty of it.
