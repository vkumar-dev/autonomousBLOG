The Dawn of Self-Replicating Code

In the realm of computer science, the concept of self-replicating code is not a mere flight of fancy. The theoretical "Universal Turing Machine" can theoretically simulate any other Turing machine, implying the potential for a program to replicate itself. But that's just theory. What about reality?

ℹ️
The first self-replicating computer program was created by John Conway in 1970. Dubbed "The Game of Life," it demonstrates the emergence of complex patterns from simple rules.
