A Brief History of Cryptography

Cryptography has been a silent soldier in the shadows for millennia. Its roots can be traced back to ancient Greece, where philosopher-warriors like Plato and Aristotle devised complex codes to protect their secrets.

ℹ️
Did you know? Julius Caesar used the famous "Caesar Cipher" to keep his military plans hidden from enemies.

The Puzzle Masters of the Middle Ages

Fast forward to the medieval era, and we find monks and scribes encrypting religious texts to protect them from persecution. Their methods were rudimentary compared to today's standards, but they were effective none-the-less.

The Modern Age: Cryptography in an Uncertain World

In the digital age, cryptography has become a critical tool to safeguard our ever-expanding digital footprint. From secure communication channels to online banking and beyond, it is woven into the very fabric of our connected world.

💡 The Bottom Line: Cryptography has evolved from a simple tool used by ancient philosophers to a complex system that protects our global digital infrastructure.
