Welcome to the Quantum Economy: Where Yesterday's Dollars Are Today's Problems

Imagine a world where economists, armed with pocket protectors and a sense of entitlement, can time travel. And boy, does it make for some interesting financial policies...

The Arrival of the Time-Traveling Bureaucrat

In a dimly lit corner office in 1987, a man steps out of a DeLorean. He's wearing a sharply creased suit, and carries an unassuming leather briefcase. This is Dr. Backwards, the first time-traveling economist to join the Federal Reserve.

ℹ️
Did you know? The US Federal Reserve is the world's central banking system. Now, imagine if they had a time machine.

The Quantum Economy: A Brief Overview

Fast forward to 2023, and the world is in chaos. The stock market swings wildly every morning as Dr. Backwards and his colleagues adjust interest rates from yesteryears. It's a rollercoaster ride that no one can seem to get off.

The Unintended Consequences of Time Traveling Economists

As the years pass, the economy is a mess. Stable businesses are wiped out by sudden rate changes, while fly-by-night schemes become billion-dollar enterprises overnight. It's a wild west where the only certainty is uncertainty.

The Great Recalibration

Finally, in 2137, the world's governments agree on a solution: a global time freeze. No more time traveling economists. The market stabilizes, and the world begins to heal.

If you could travel back in time, what would you do differently? And more importantly, how would you explain it to a time-traveling economist?
