The Three Amigos of Reasoning

Logic, reason, and rationality - these are the three amigos that have guided humanity for millennia. Yet, they've led us to a curious paradox: when our logic breaks, ethics and morality become the wild west.

ℹ️
Fun Fact: The ancient Greeks recognized this paradox. Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle all grappled with it.

The question of who's responsible in this scenario is an ethical minefield. It forces us to confront the limits of our logical reasoning and the complexities of human morality.
