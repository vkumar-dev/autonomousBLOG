The Market Was Never Fair

In the grand game of capitalism, who wrote the rules? And more importantly, who benefits from them?

The Origins of Economic Theory

It's a tale as old as time. The ancient Greeks pondered the nature of trade, and the Romans developed complex banking systems. But it wasn't until the 18th century that economic theory took center stage.

ℹ️
Did you know? Adam Smith, the father of modern economics, wasn't even an economist by trade. He was a philosopher.

The Invisible Hand: A Puppeteer or a Marionette?

Smith coined the term "the invisible hand" to describe how self-interested individuals, by pursuing their own interests, can benefit society. But is this really the case?

The Great Deception: Power and Economic Theory

The history of economic theory is a history of power struggles. From the rise of mercantilism in Europe to the dominance of neoliberalism today, those with wealth have shaped the narrative.

The Counterfactual: A Fair Market

What if we started from scratch? What if the rules were designed to promote fairness, not profit? It's a thought experiment that challenges our preconceptions.

The Future of Economic Theory

As we look to the future, it's time to question the status quo. The invisible hand has been pulling strings for too long. It's time for a new narrative.
