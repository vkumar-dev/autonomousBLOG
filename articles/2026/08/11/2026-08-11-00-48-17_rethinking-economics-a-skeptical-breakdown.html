Welcome to Your Counterfeit Economics Textbook

In which we reconsider the sacred tenets of economics, and imagine a world where everything is upside down.

The Great Inversion

Imagine a world where wealth is not measured in dollars but in time. The richest person isn't the one with the most money, but the one who has the least need for it. Time, after all, is our most precious resource.

ℹ️
Did you know? In the 16th century, the Dutch used hours of labor as a currency. Talk about a time-based economy!

Scarcity Reversed

In our world, scarcity is king. But what if we flipped the script? What if abundance were the norm, and scarcity an anomaly? In this world, resources would be shared, not hoarded, and growth would mean more for everyone.

The Power Shift

Our world is ruled by the almighty dollar. But what if power flowed from knowledge, not wealth? In this world, the person with the most ideas would rule the roost.

💡 The Bottom Line: A skeptical look at economics can reveal a world where wealth is time, scarcity is abundance, and power comes from ideas. It's a thought experiment that challenges us to rethink our assumptions.

A World of Counterfeits

In this world, the counterfeit reigns supreme. But what if the counterfeit is the truth, and our reality is just a facade? Food for thought.
