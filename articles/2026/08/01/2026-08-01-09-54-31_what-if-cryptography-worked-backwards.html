Cryptographers, once vilified, would be the new rockstars. Their skills could unlock the secrets of the past and propel humanity into a future where privacy is a relic.

The Psychological Implications

This world would force us to reconsider our relationship with secrecy. Would we value privacy less, knowing its cost? Or would we become more secretive, fearing the ever-watchful eye of decryption?

💡 The Bottom Line: The nature of secrets is intrinsically linked to our sense of self. A world where cryptography works backwards would challenge our understanding of privacy and identity.
