Welcome to the Anti-AI Game Design Universe

In a bizarre twist of cosmic irony, AI has been outlawed in game design. Welcome to the wild west of human-led creativity, where every game is a testament to our greatest triumphs and darkest fears.

The First Level: The Simplest of Simulations

In this universe, game design starts as it did in ours - with the humble pixel. But in this world, every pixel is painstakingly placed by human hands, not the cold, calculating algorithms of AI. The results are gloriously messy, with games that are as much art as they are entertainment.

ℹ️
Did you know? The first video game, "Tennis for Two", was programmed by a human - not an AI.

The Middle Levels: The Brain-Bending Branches

As game design evolved, the complexity of games increased. But without AI to handle the calculations, the human mind had to step up to the challenge. Games became labyrinthine, with branching paths that required players to think critically and strategize to succeed.

The Final Levels: The Mind-Bending Maze

At the pinnacle of game design, there lies the ultimate test - a game so complex, so impossible to solve without human intuition, that it is said to have driven many mad. It's a game of infinite possibilities, where every decision leads to a different outcome. And it's all because in this universe, the only intelligence allowed is the human kind.

💡 The Bottom Line: In this alternate universe, game design is a testament to the power of human creativity and the complexity of the human mind. It's a reminder that even in a world without AI, we can still create wonders.

The Unanswered Question

So, if AI were banned from game design, what would our games look like? Would they be better, worse, or just different? The answer, like any good game, remains to be discovered.
