The Final Act: The Conscious Killer Strikes

In a dystopian future, a killer is at large. Not just any killer, but one who can control the very fabric of reality itself. The only clue: a single strand of hair, seemingly untouched by the passage of time.

The human hair grows at a rate of 0.5 inches per month. So a strand can reveal the time of a crime with pinpoint accuracy, right? Wrong.

The First Clue: The Dance of the DNA

The hair is sent to the lab for DNA analysis. The results? A perfect match to a known criminal, who was supposed to be in prison at the time of the murder. How could this be?

The First Scene: The Dawn of Forensics

The story of forensics is as old as the story of crime itself. It began with simple fingerprints and bloodhounds, and it has evolved into a complex tapestry of science, technology, and human intuition.

💡 The Bottom Line: Forensics is far from infallible. Each piece of evidence must be approached with a critical eye, and each conclusion must be backed by solid science.
