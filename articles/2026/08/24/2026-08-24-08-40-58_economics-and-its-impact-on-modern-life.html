The Age of Barter: When Life was a Bargain Bin

Before the invention of money, life was a wild game of "I'll scratch your back if you scratch mine." The ancient Greeks traded goats for wine, the Romans dealt in salt, and the Aztecs preferred cocoa beans. It's a wonder they ever managed to conquer the world, what with all the caffeine-induced frenzies.

Did you know? The first form of currency was a cow, in the ancient city of Mohenjo-daro, around 2300 BC.

The Great Depression: The Economy's Meltdown

In the 1930s, the world's richest countries plunged into a deep recession. Banks collapsed, and unemployment soared. It was like watching a circus, but instead of laughing, people were crying in their popcorn.
