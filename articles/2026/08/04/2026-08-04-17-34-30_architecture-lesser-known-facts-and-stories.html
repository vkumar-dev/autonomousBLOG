The Labyrinthine Library of Croton

In the ancient city of Croton, there stood a library that defied logic. With its seemingly infinite corridors and countless books, it was said to contain all knowledge, yet its architect never left a blueprint behind.

ℹ️
This library's design is still debated among architectural historians today, making it one of the greatest unsolved mysteries in the field.

The Floating Mosque of Yonghe

In Yonghe, China, there exists a mosque that appears to defy gravity. At certain angles and under specific lighting conditions, it seems to float above the ground. Yet, the architect's design notes reveal no such illusion.

The Shrinking House of Penzance

In the quaint town of Penzance, England, there's a house that shrinks. Every hundred years or so, it seems to lose a few feet in size. But why? And how is this possible? The answer lies in the intricate mathematics hidden within its walls.

The Infinite Staircase of Hildesheim

In the German city of Hildesheim, there's a staircase that seems to go on forever. Yet, if you were to count its steps, you would find it has an odd number. A paradox, to be sure, but one that invites us to question our understanding of space and time.
