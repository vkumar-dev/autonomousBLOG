The Discovery of Time Travel: A Pandora's Box

Upon the discovery of time travel, humanity was faced with an existential crisis. With the ability to alter history at will, the very fabric of reality became unstable. It was then that a brilliant cryptographer named Amelia X developed a solution: temporal encryption.

Temporal Encryption: A Lifeline for Reality

Temporal encryption, as it came to be known, was a revolutionary concept. By encrypting their origins, time travelers could ensure that they did not alter the past in unintended ways. This cryptographic technology became the cornerstone of temporal ethics.

The Cat and Mouse Game: Time Travelers vs Cryptographers

As time travel advanced, so did temporal encryption. A cat-and-mouse game ensued between time travelers, constantly evolving their methods to avoid detection, and the cryptographers who sought to keep history intact.
