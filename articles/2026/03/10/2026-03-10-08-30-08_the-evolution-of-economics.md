The origins of economics can be traced back to ancient civilizations, where philosophers like Aristotle and later economists like Adam Smith and Karl Marx, laid the foundations for modern economic theory.

> "The wealth of nations is not an accidental product of nature, but something that can be cultivated by men of foresight and enterprise." - Adam Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*
