## Time as a Linguistic Construct

Historians often grapple with the concept of time, particularly in its linear representation. Yet, in a world where AI-driven language models are commonplace, we can reconsider time as a linguistic construct—a series of words strung together to form narratives. By understanding this connection between language and time, historians can more accurately interpret past events and predict future trends.

In a futuristic setting, advancements in AI and machine learning could help historians sift through data more efficiently while minimizing the risk of error or manipulation.
